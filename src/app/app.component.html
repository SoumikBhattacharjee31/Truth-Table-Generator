Quick guide

How expressions work

  1. 1

    Use a single letter for each variable. Uppercase and lowercase letters are distinct.

  2. 2

    Put the apostrophe after what you want to negate: A' or (A+B)'.

  3. 3

    NOT is evaluated first, AND and XOR next, then OR. Parentheses override that order.
